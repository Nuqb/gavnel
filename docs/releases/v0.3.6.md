# Gavnel v0.3.6

- Window buttons now sit inside Gavnel. Drag the empty header area to move the app, double-click it to maximize or restore, and resize from the edges or corners.
- The playback timeline and controls stay attached to the app during window movement and transitions. Playback also gains fullscreen controls and refined seeking and volume controls.
- Save edited videos directly: replace the current video or save a new MP4, with resolution and file-size settings. Saves can continue in the background, with progress and completion notices.
- Rename recordings and screenshots from the library while keeping companion metadata together.
- Compatible trims can copy compressed media without re-encoding. Other edits use Windows-native rendering. Timeline thumbnails now appear progressively.
- Export staging and replacement recovery protect existing captures, and active saves block conflicting changes and app exit.
- The quick launcher now switches older source builds through the normal graceful restart process.

The installer preserves settings and recordings. Windows 11 x64, SDR capture, and an NVIDIA GPU with compatible NVENC drivers are required for recording. No new recording-performance claim is made. The Windows installer is not Authenticode-signed.
