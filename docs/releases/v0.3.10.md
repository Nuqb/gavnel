# Gavnel v0.3.10

- Fixed clips recorded with Separate Audio Tracks playing only your microphone. Gavnel and the Windows player now play the combined PC audio and microphone mix, including in Gavnel for clips you already recorded.
- Fixed the editor showing the wrong separate audio tracks. PC Audio and Microphone are now labeled correctly.
- Added notifications when a long recording starts, is saved, or can't be saved.

Clips recorded with Separate Audio Tracks before this update still start on the microphone track in the Windows player; they play correctly in Gavnel.

One x64 installer supports Windows 10 22H2 (build 19045) and Windows 11. Recording requires an SDR source and an NVIDIA GPU with compatible NVENC drivers. Game-only and selected-app audio require Windows 11. Settings and recordings are preserved during updates. The installer is not Authenticode-signed.
